# CompactCapture Privacy Policy

**English** | [日本語](PRIVACY_POLICY_ja.md)

Last updated: August 9, 2026

CompactCapture (the “App”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

## 1. Information we collect

The current version of the App does not collect personal information, usage data, advertising identifiers, location information, contacts, or other user data. The App has no account registration, analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking, or transmission of data to an external server.

## 2. Camera and microphone

The App uses the camera to capture photos and videos. It uses the microphone when you record video with audio enabled. Camera and microphone data is used only to perform the capture requested by the user.

## 3. Photo and video storage

By default, captured photos and videos are stored locally in the App's private container on the device. An item is added to the system Photos library only when the user enables the Photos option or explicitly chooses to export an item from its detail screen.

The App requests add-only Photos permission. It does not request permission to read existing items in the user's Photos library.

## 4. Sharing with third parties

Because the App does not collect user data, it does not sell, share, or provide user data to third parties.

## 5. Data management and deletion

Photos and videos stored in the App's private container can be deleted from the App's library or detail screen. Deleting the App also deletes data that remains only in the App's private container.

Copies added to the system Photos library are managed separately. Deleting an item from the App, or deleting the App itself, does not delete a copy that was previously added to Photos. Delete such copies separately in the Photos app if needed.

## 6. Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from users of any age.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If a future feature changes how information is handled, this page and its “Last updated” date will be revised.

## 8. Contact

For questions about this policy or the App, open an issue in the [CompactCapture GitHub repository](https://github.com/early277/CompactCapture/issues).
